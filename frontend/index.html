What the colours mean

A✓ Right letter, right spot
B↔ In the word, wrong spot
C✕ Not in the word

How to play

  1. Guess the hidden five-letter word. You have 8 tries.
  2. Type with your keyboard or tap the letters on screen, then press Check word (or the Enter key). On a phone or tablet, tap the letter boxes to bring up your own keyboard.
  3. Stuck? Press Hint and I'll tell you a letter from the word.
  4. After each guess, the tiles change colour:
    • A✓ Green with a tick – right letter, right spot.
    • B↔ Gold with arrows – the letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
    • C✕ Grey with a cross – the letter is not in the word.

Playing with your voice

Tap Tap to speak (or press the space bar), then say things like:

  • “Guess crane” or spell it: “C, R, A, N, E”
  • “Letter A” · “Delete” · “Clear the row” · “Enter”
  • “Read my guesses” · “Which letters have I used?”
  • “Give me a hint” · “How many guesses left?”
  • “Help” · “Say that again” · “Speak slower”
  • “New game”
